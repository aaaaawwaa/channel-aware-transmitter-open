Phase 3 -- C_ATT: does the scope still read a KNOWN 20000 mdB step as 20 dB?
==============================================================================
  This is the phase that decides HOW TO READ Phase 2.  A drop in the
  Phase 2 column has two possible authors -- the TX chain rolling off,
  or the scope losing amplitude accuracy -- and they are only
  separable by injecting a step whose size is known independently.
  Where C_ATT holds, the scope's amplitude reading is sound and the
  Phase 2 drop is the SOURCE.  Where C_ATT cannot be measured at all,
  the Phase 2 number there is not evidence of anything.

  negative case has to be about the SOURCE: at maximum attenuation
